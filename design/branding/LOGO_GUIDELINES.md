# Logo Guidelines

> Status: Draft
> Version: 1.0.0
> Owner: Brand Team
> Last Updated: 2026-07-29

---

# Purpose

This document defines the principles, usage rules, and governance for the ProjectEcho visual identity.

The logo should represent ProjectEcho's mission of transforming evidence into trusted career intelligence while remaining simple, memorable, and scalable.

---

# Brand Identity Philosophy

ProjectEcho represents:

- Growth through evidence
- Career intelligence
- Continuous improvement
- Transparency
- Trust
- Professional development

The visual identity should communicate these values without unnecessary complexity.

---

# Logo Objectives

The logo should be:

- Simple
- Memorable
- Timeless
- Scalable
- Professional
- Distinctive
- Accessible

It should work equally well in product interfaces, documentation, presentations, websites, and social media.

---

# Brand Mark

The preferred logo should combine:

- Symbol
- Wordmark

The symbol should be recognizable on its own for use as:

- App icon
- Browser favicon
- Mobile icon
- Social media avatar

---

# Symbol Philosophy

The symbol should visually represent one or more of the following concepts:

- Echo
- Knowledge
- Intelligence
- Growth
- Connection
- Evidence
- Network
- Career progression

Avoid literal resume, CV, or document icons.

The symbol should remain meaningful even without accompanying text.

---

# Wordmark

The wordmark should use a clean, modern, highly readable typeface.

Characteristics:

- Professional
- Minimal
- Geometric
- Human-friendly

The wordmark should never overpower the symbol.

---

# Logo Variants

Maintain the following official variants:

## Primary Logo

Symbol + Wordmark

---

## Horizontal Logo

For navigation bars and websites.

---

## Stacked Logo

For presentations and marketing material.

---

## Symbol Only

For:

- App icon
- Favicon
- Social media
- Small UI surfaces

---

# Clear Space

Maintain clear space around the logo equal to the height of the symbol.

No text or graphical element should enter this area.

---

# Minimum Size

Digital:

- Full logo: minimum 120 px wide
- Symbol: minimum 24 px

Print:

- Full logo: minimum 25 mm

---

# Color Usage

The logo should support:

- Full color
- Monochrome
- White
- Dark mode

The logo should always maintain sufficient contrast against its background.

---

# Background Usage

Preferred:

- White
- Neutral
- Dark surfaces

Avoid placing the logo on visually busy backgrounds.

---

# Incorrect Usage

Never:

- Stretch
- Distort
- Rotate
- Add shadows
- Add gradients unless officially defined
- Change proportions
- Change typography
- Add outlines
- Use unapproved colors

---

# App Icon

The app icon should:

- Use only the symbol
- Be recognizable at small sizes
- Remain legible at 16×16 pixels
- Avoid unnecessary detail

---

# Favicon

Use the simplified symbol only.

No text.

---

# Social Media

Profile images should use the symbol centered within a square format.

Maintain generous padding.

---

# Accessibility

The logo should:

- Meet contrast requirements
- Remain identifiable at small sizes
- Avoid conveying meaning solely through color

---

# Future Evolution

Future brand refreshes should preserve:

- Recognition
- Simplicity
- Trustworthiness
- Consistency

Major logo redesigns should be accompanied by an updated Brand Guide and migration plan.

---

# Governance

Any modification to the official logo, symbol, wordmark, or brand assets must be reviewed and approved through the ProjectEcho design governance process.

Unofficial variants should not be used in production.
