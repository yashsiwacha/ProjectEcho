# Purpose

This document defines the primary user personas for ProjectEcho.

Personas describe the goals, motivations, pain points, and expected workflows of the people using the platform. Every feature, interaction, and recommendation should be evaluated against these personas.

---

# Primary Persona: Early Career Software Engineer

## Profile

- Student or recent graduate
- 0–3 years of experience
- Actively building projects
- Preparing for internships or full-time roles

## Goals

- Understand career readiness
- Identify strengths and weaknesses
- Build a strong portfolio
- Receive personalized learning recommendations
- Track measurable progress

## Pain Points

- Unsure which skills matter most
- Generic learning resources
- Difficult to showcase practical competency
- Resume doesn't reflect real abilities
- Limited feedback on career growth

## Success Criteria

- Increased Career Readiness Score
- Better interview performance
- Strong evidence-backed Career Passport
- Personalized improvement roadmap

---

# Secondary Persona: Experienced Professional

## Profile

- 3–10 years of experience
- Looking for career advancement
- Interested in leadership or specialization

## Goals

- Identify skill gaps
- Plan career transitions
- Validate expertise
- Track long-term growth

## Pain Points

- Difficult to evaluate changing market expectations
- Limited visibility into transferable skills
- Static resumes become outdated

## Success Criteria

- Clear development roadmap
- Evidence-backed competency profile
- Increased confidence in career decisions

---

# Recruiter

## Profile

- Hiring manager or recruiter
- Reviews candidates at scale

## Goals

- Verify competency quickly
- Reduce hiring uncertainty
- Understand candidate strengths

## Pain Points

- Resumes lack evidence
- Difficult to compare candidates objectively
- Limited insight into practical ability

## Success Criteria

- Faster candidate evaluation
- Higher confidence in hiring decisions
- Transparent competency evidence

---

# Mentor / Career Coach

## Profile

- Mentor, educator, or coach
- Supports learners and professionals

## Goals

- Track mentee progress
- Recommend improvements
- Identify learning gaps

## Pain Points

- Limited visibility into actual competency
- Difficult to measure progress objectively

## Success Criteria

- Better coaching outcomes
- Clear competency tracking
- Actionable recommendations

# Persona Prioritization

## MVP

1. Early Career Software Engineer

## Phase 2

2. Experienced Professional

## Phase 3

3. Recruiter
4. Mentor / Career Coach

---

# Design Implications

- Evidence should always be visible.
- AI recommendations must be explainable.
- Progress should be measurable over time.
- Navigation should prioritize clarity over complexity.
- Mobile workflows should focus on quick updates and evidence capture.
