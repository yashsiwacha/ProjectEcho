## Purpose
Founder Decision Records (FDRs) exist to establish the highest level of governance and strategic direction for ProjectEcho. They form the absolute root of the repository's authority chain. 

This specific decision is required to formally resolve a critical P0 governance conflict regarding the fundamental product identity of the repository, enabling downstream architecture and engineering work to resume with absolute certainty.

---

## Background
The Repository Intelligence Audit (RIA v1) and subsequent Conflict Evidence Report identified a Priority P0 conflict (CR-001). The repository currently documents two mutually exclusive product identities: a Career Intelligence Platform and an Event Delivery/Webhook Backbone. The repository cannot proceed safely until one direction is formally enshrined as the sole product identity, as all architectural and implementation decisions depend entirely on the target domain.

---

## Evidence
The conflict was verified through the following repository evidence:
- **`PROJECT_MANIFEST.md` and `README.md`**: Both explicitly define ProjectEcho as a "Career Intelligence Platform replacing static resumes with evidence-driven, explainable career profiles."
- **`ADR-0002`**: A Frozen architecture document built upon the assumptions of a Career Intelligence domain.
- **`docs/PROJECT_VISION.md`**: Explicitly defines ProjectEcho as an "event-driven platform for receiving, processing, routing, and making operational events observable."
- **Conflict Evidence Report**: Confirmed that these dual identities actively contradict each other, and noted that without a founder-level decision, neither document has the necessary authority to supersede the other.

---

## Decision
**ProjectEcho is formally established as a Career Intelligence Platform.** 

The earlier repository documentation representing an Event Processing / Event Delivery Backbone was an exploratory phase. It is no longer the active product direction. The Career Intelligence Platform is the sole, authoritative product identity for this repository.

---

## Scope
This decision governs:
- **Product Identity:** The fundamental nature of the product being built.
- **Mission & Vision:** The strategic goals of the repository.
- **Primary Users:** The target audience for the platform.
- **Repository Direction:** The overarching roadmap for all teams.
- **Architecture Assumptions:** The business domain that all future architecture must support.

## Out of Scope
This decision does **not** govern:
- Software Architecture
- Backend Design
- Infrastructure Topology
- Technology Choices
- Implementation Details

---

## Consequences
The immediate consequences of this decision are:
1. `PROJECT_MANIFEST.md` becomes the single, authoritative source of truth regarding the product's identity.
2. `docs/PROJECT_VISION.md` (and related event-delivery documentation) is formally stripped of active governance authority and becomes a historical artifact.
3. All future governance documents must inherit from and support the Career Intelligence identity.
4. All future architecture designs must assume the Career Intelligence domain.

---

## Required Follow-up
To re-establish a healthy governance chain and resume engineering, the following sequence must be executed in order:
1. **Framework Governance Model (FGM):** Draft and ratify the governance rules.
2. **Career Intelligence Framework (CIF):** Define the domain terminology and logic.
3. **Engineering Architecture Framework (EAF):** Ratify the architectural principles.
4. **Engineering Architecture Document (EAD):** Finalize the system architecture.
5. **Backend Alignment Review:** Audit and refactor the current codebase against the newly approved EAD.

---

## Repository Actions
To align the repository with this decision, the following actions are recommended:
- **Archive, Do Not Delete:** Move `docs/PROJECT_VISION.md`, `docs/ENGINEERING_GUIDE.md`, and any related legacy architecture documents into `docs/archive/`. Do not delete historical context.
- **Update Documentation:** Ensure that the `README.md` and `PROJECT_MANIFEST.md` reflect this resolved identity and link to this FDR as the authoritative source.

---

## Governance Rules
By the authority of the Founders, the following governance rules are established:
1. **FDR Supremacy:** Founder Decision Records sit above every other governance artifact. Nothing below an FDR may contradict it.
2. **Supersession:** Future FDRs may supersede previous FDRs. ADRs, ARBRs, and Frameworks cannot override an FDR.
3. **Implementation Boundaries:** Engineering implementation cannot redefine the product identity or ignore the constraints established herein.
